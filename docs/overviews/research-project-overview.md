## Headline
- 26 abilities catalogued; 10 at High certainty.
- Strength vs certainty: Spearman rho = -0.60 (p ~ 0.05, n = 11); strength vs prevalence: rho = -0.81 (p = 0.002).
- Every High-certainty ability works through a lever (feedback, breathing, muscle tension, imagery, suggestion).
- The one Extreme, High-certainty, common ability: tensor tympani contraction (~43% self-report; +22 dB at 250 Hz).
- No documented unaided mind-to-mind or mind-to-another's-nerve link; hardware links (Warwick 2002, brain-spine interface 2023, brain-to-brain 2014/2019) are documented.

## Proposed study (18 months)
- Phase 1: pre-registered systematic review re-extracting all abilities on the common SD scale (PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO).
- Phase 2: topic-blind survey (n = 2,000) -> lab verification (n = 240: tympanometry, video-oculography, auricular video/EMG, GooseLab piloerection, pupillometry; co-registered EMG lever check) -> training pilot (n = 60, feedback vs sham) -> family verification (n = 100).
- Pre-registered hypotheses H1-H7 and sample-size justification are in the full document.

## Open decision
Primary study target: lever-free effectors (assumed) vs an independent sham-controlled replication of the breathing-cytokine effect.
