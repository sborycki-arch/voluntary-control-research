# Voluntary body control: evidence map

Compiled 2026-10-03. Interactive 3D map: https://claude.ai/artifact/HDVbejcMxEMmH8LBqoiEci

## Rubrics

- **Certainty** (does the control exist, not clinical benefit): High = objectively measured, replicated by independent groups or meta-analysis, mechanism understood; Moderate = objective and controlled but small samples or mostly one group; Low = one study, a handful of people, or not peer-reviewed; Not supported = tested and not shown.
- **Strength vs mean** = change ÷ population SD in healthy adults at rest (or the source's standardized effect size). None = no change; Small < 0.5 SD; Moderate 0.5–1; Large 1–3; Extreme > 3. Percent-only results: Small < 10%, Moderate 10–30%, Large 30–60%, Extreme > 60%. On/off = a silent muscle switches on, no SD at rest.
- **Who can do it**: None · Single cases (≤3) · Adepts (small expert group, rate unmeasured) · Rare (<5%, measured) · Minority (5–50%, measured) · Most, with training.

## Correlation

- Strength vs certainty: Spearman ρ = -0.6 (p ≈ 0.05, n = 11).
- Strength vs who can do it: ρ = -0.81 (p = 0.002).
- Built-in bias: Low-certainty heights are best individuals; High-certainty heights are group averages. Exception: middle-ear muscle (Extreme, High certainty, ~43%).

- Resting heart rate: 65.5 ± 7.7 bpm — Quer et al. 2020, PLoS ONE (n = 92,457)
- Systolic BP: 122 ± 17 mmHg — NHANES 2001–08 (Wright et al. 2011, NHSR 35); SD derived from 5th–95th and IQR percentiles
- Armpit temperature: 35.97 ± 0.48 °C — Geneva et al. 2019, Open Forum Infect Dis (axillary)
- Left–right hand temperature difference: 0.31 ± 0.25 °C — Uematsu et al. 1988, J Neurosurg (hand, n = 56)
- Finger skin temperature: 27.16 ± 3.2 °C — Sci Rep 2019 thermography, healthy controls at 23 °C room
- Pupil diameter, normal light: 4.03 ± 0.9 mm — Schröder et al. 2018, Eur J Ophthalmol (photopic, n = 91)
- Hearing threshold, 250 Hz: SD 5.0 dB — Shahnaz & Ciocca 2026, Hear Res (250 Hz, n = 126)
