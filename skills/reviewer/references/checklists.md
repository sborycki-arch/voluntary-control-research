# Checklists applied by the reviewer

## PRISMA 2020 (Page et al. 2021, BMJ 372:n71) — item numbers and titles
1 Title · 2 Abstract · 3 Rationale · 4 Objectives · 5 Eligibility criteria · 6 Information sources · 7 Search strategy · 8 Selection process · 9 Data collection process · 10a Outcomes, 10b Other variables · 11 Study risk of bias assessment · 12 Effect measures · 13a–f Synthesis methods · 14 Reporting bias assessment · 15 Certainty assessment · 16a–b Study selection · 17 Study characteristics · 18 Risk of bias in studies · 19 Results of individual studies · 20a–d Results of syntheses · 21 Reporting biases · 22 Certainty of evidence · 23a–d Discussion · 24a–c Registration and protocol · 25 Support · 26 Competing interests · 27 Availability of data, code and other materials.
Items 8, 9 and 11 must state that two independent agents performed the step, how agreement was measured, and that a human adjudicated.

## PRISMA-P (protocol, Gate 2)
Shamseer et al. 2015, BMJ 349:g7647 — 17 items; the search strategy for at least one database is given in full.

## PRISMA-trAIce (AI as a review tool; JMIR AI 2025, e80247)
Apply the published checklist items on: tool identity and version, stage of use, prompts and configuration, validation performed, human oversight, error handling, and limitations. Read the published item list; do not work from memory.

## Ding et al. 2026 (arXiv 2608.05179), Table 10 measured items
- Human-in-the-loop entry points stated (list of points).
- Code released (runnable repository).
- Seeds or execution traces released (re-runnable artefact).
- Novelty-verification method stated (method plus a sampled check) — for this review, the gap statement's database searches.
- Number of attempts and the selection policy stated.
The three proposed items are to be quoted from the paper's PDF before use.

## RAISE 2026 v3 (doi:10.17605/OSF.IO/FWAUD) — evidence-synthesist items
Tool name, version and dates; purpose and stages affected; how it was used; justification and validation; inputs and outputs shared where feasible; human remains responsible.

## BARG (Kruschke 2021, Nat Hum Behav 5:1282–1291) — Gates 5 and 6
Priors stated and justified; prior predictive check; model specification; MCMC diagnostics (R-hat, effective sample size); posterior summaries with credible intervals; decision thresholds stated in advance; prior sensitivity; code and data available.

## ICMJE (January 2026, Section II.A.4 and Section V) — Gate 7
AI use disclosed in the cover letter and in the manuscript (methods for data collection and analysis; acknowledgments for writing assistance); no AI author; human authors responsible for all content.
